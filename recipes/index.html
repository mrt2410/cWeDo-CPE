How to use a recipe
  1. Press Download on a recipe (or View JSON and copy it into a file named something.wedo.json).
  2. In the app, click the folder icon (Open) and choose the file.
  3. Press the green flag on the Start block. Hardware a recipe needs is listed on its card.
